## Template Notes

- Name should be concise and not marketing-style.
- Background should state related technology and objective defects.
- Technical solution should describe structures, relationships, process, and
  working principle.
- Protected technical points should be ordered by importance.
- Beneficial effects should connect technical means to mechanism, solved
  problem, and technical effect.
- Drawings should clearly show the method/system, including flowcharts when
  appropriate.
